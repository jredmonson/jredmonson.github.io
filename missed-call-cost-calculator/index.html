Plumbers, electricians, HVAC contractors, roofers, garage door companies, salons, and independent contractors all run into the same wall: the person calling isn't shopping around because they distrust you — they're shopping around because you didn't answer. Speed matters more than almost anything else in that moment. Industry benchmarks put the number at roughly 62% of inbound calls going unanswered for small businesses without a dedicated receptionist, and higher for solo operators and crews who are on job sites most of the day. Your own rate is almost certainly different, which is exactly why the worksheet above uses your real numbers instead of that average. What's the difference between a missed-call text-back and an AI voice receptionist? A missed-call text-back sends an automatic SMS the moment a call goes unanswered, so the caller gets a response even if nobody's available. An AI voice receptionist actually answers the call live and can book the job on the spot. Most local service businesses eventually run both: text-back for after-hours and overflow, AI answering for calls during business hours. Does missed-call text-back really work for home service businesses like plumbing or HVAC? Yes — it tends to perform especially well for trades, because the caller usually has an urgent problem such as a leak or no AC, and will respond to a text asking what they need rather than waiting on hold or calling a competitor.
