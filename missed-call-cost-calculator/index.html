03 — Why this keeps happening

A ringing phone feels like a small thing to miss — you were on a roof, under a sink, mid-appointment with someone who already paid you. But the caller on the other end doesn't know that. They just know nobody picked up, and they have four more numbers to try.

None of this is unique to any one trade. Plumbers, electricians, HVAC contractors, roofers, garage door companies, salons, and independent contractors all run into the same wall: the person calling isn't shopping around because they distrust you — they're shopping around because you didn't answer. Speed matters more than almost anything else in that moment. Research from Harvard Business Review and MIT found that businesses responding to a new lead within five minutes are roughly 100 times more likely to actually make contact than those who wait half an hour, and 78% of customers end up hiring whoever answers first, not whoever quoted the lowest price. That's the real cost of a missed call: it's rarely that the customer decided not to buy. It's that somebody else's phone got answered first.

06 — Frequently asked questions

How many calls does the average small business actually miss?

Industry benchmarks put the number at roughly 62% of inbound calls going unanswered for small businesses without a dedicated receptionist, and higher for solo operators and crews who are on job sites most of the day. Your own rate is almost certainly different, which is exactly why the worksheet above uses your real numbers instead of that average.

What's the difference between a missed-call text-back and an AI voice receptionist?

A missed-call text-back sends an automatic SMS the moment a call goes unanswered, so the caller gets a response even if nobody's available. An AI voice receptionist actually answers the call live and can book the job on the spot. Most local service businesses eventually run both: text-back for after-hours and overflow, AI answering for calls during business hours.

Does missed-call text-back really work for home service businesses like plumbing or HVAC?

Yes — it tends to perform especially well for trades, because the caller usually has an urgent problem such as a leak or no AC, and will respond to a text asking what they need rather than waiting on hold or calling a competitor.

How accurate is this missed call cost calculator?

It's a planning estimate, not an audit. It applies published industry benchmarks for call-abandonment and text-back response rates to the numbers you enter. The inputs matter more than the assumptions — the more honestly you estimate your own call volume and close rate, the more useful the output.

How fast can a missed-call text-back actually go live?

For most local service businesses, a missed-call instant text-back can be set up and running on your existing business number within about a week — no new phone system and no additional hires required.
