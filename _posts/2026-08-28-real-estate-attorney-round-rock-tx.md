## Why New-Construction Contracts in Round Rock Deserve Extra Scrutiny

Builder-drafted contracts are written to protect the builder's interests by default - provisions around construction delays, design changes, and what happens if a buyer wants to back out are often less favorable to the buyer than a standard resale contract negotiated between two individual parties. A title company handling the closing generally isn't reviewing those terms for fairness; that review is specifically an attorney's job (or a covered benefit under a legal plan), not something to assume is already being handled.

Buyers in Round Rock's newer developments should specifically look at how the contract handles earnest money if the closing is delayed by the builder, what recourse exists if the finished home doesn't match agreed-upon specifications, and whether there are any binding arbitration clauses that would limit the buyer's options if a dispute arises later.

## Williamson County Recording and Closing Norms
