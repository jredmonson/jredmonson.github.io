### Step 2: Set up your agency sub-account snapshot
A "snapshot" is a pre-built GHL setup — workflows, pipelines, email sequences, forms — that you can deploy into any new client account in minutes. Building a great snapshot once means you can onboard a new client in an afternoon instead of weeks.

GHL has industry-specific snapshots in their marketplace. Start with one that matches your target niche (e.g., dentist, home services, real estate).

### Step 3: White-label the platform
Under your agency settings, add your:
- Agency name and logo
- Custom domain (e.g., crm.youragency.com)
- Support email
- Brand colors

Your clients log in and see your brand, not GoHighLevel's.

### Step 4: Set up your own agency's funnel and CRM
Before you can help clients capture leads, you need a funnel that captures leads for you. Use GHL to build a simple landing page that describes your service, an intake form, and a follow-up automation sequence.

### Step 5: Connect your payment processing
GHL integrates with Stripe directly. Set up your subscription products (your monthly retainer packages) so clients can pay you automatically.

## How to Get Your First Local Business Client {#get-clients}

The fastest path to your first client is not paid ads. It's conversations.

### Method 1: Local outreach with a free audit
Identify 20–30 local businesses in your chosen niche. Check their Google Business Profile — is it incomplete? Do they have reviews? Do they have a website? A booking link?

Reach out (email or phone) offering a free "digital presence audit." You're not pitching software. You're showing them what they're missing in terms they care about: leads, reviews, and booked appointments.

### Method 2: Referral chain from one anchor client
Land one client in a niche — say, a dentist. Deliver real results (more reviews, more booked appointments, better lead follow-up). Ask for one referral. Dentists know other dentists. Contractors know other contractors.
