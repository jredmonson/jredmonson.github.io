## Using LeadsLeap's Real-Tracker to Improve Results

One of LeadsLeap's most underused features is the real-tracker. Every link you create inside the platform gets tracked with visit quality data — not just raw click counts.

The tracker shows:

- **Total clicks** — all visits to your URL
- **Real clicks** — visits from human viewers with normal browser behavior
- **Bot clicks** — automated traffic flagged by the system
- **Average time on page** — how long visitors actually stayed

This matters because most free traffic sources are contaminated with bot traffic that inflates your numbers without any engagement value. When you run LeadsLeap traffic, you can see immediately what percentage of your clicks are real.

If your real-click rate is above 70%, your ad is performing well. If it dips below 50%, rewrite the headline — you are attracting accidental clicks from disinterested viewers.

The time-on-page metric is equally useful. If visitors are leaving in under 10 seconds, your landing page is not matching the expectation set by the ad. If they stay 60+ seconds, you have a qualified reader worth following up with.
