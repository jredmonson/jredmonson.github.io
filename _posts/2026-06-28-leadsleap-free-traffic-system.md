## How the LeadsLeap Credit System Works

Every free member earns credits by clicking through a 10-second timer on other members' ads. Each view earns a set number of credits based on the advertiser's bid. You then spend those credits to show your own ad.

The credit rate adjusts based on platform activity. More active members means more ad views available and a more competitive credit market. A committed free member surfing consistently can earn 100–300 credits per day — enough to send a meaningful number of visitors to a single offer.

There's also a passive earning mechanism through the LeadsLeap widget. Embed the widget on your blog or website, and you earn credits when visitors interact with it. Once it's placed, it earns without active effort on your part — one of the most underrated features on the platform.
