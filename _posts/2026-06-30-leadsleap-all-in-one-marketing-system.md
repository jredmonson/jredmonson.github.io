## Traffic Generation Tools {#traffic-generation}

LeadsLeap runs a cooperative traffic exchange where members earn credits by viewing other members' ads. Those credits are then spent to show your own ads to other active members.

### How the traffic co-op works:

1. You join the platform and set up your ad (usually a landing page URL)
2. You view other members' ads to earn credits
3. Your ad is shown to other members using their credits
4. Members can also earn credits passively by running the LeadsLeap widget on their site

This is "surf traffic" — opt-in, human-driven, but not targeted the way Google Ads or Facebook Ads would be. The audience is primarily marketers and work-from-home opportunity seekers, which makes it a solid fit for promoting affiliate marketing tools, digital products, and business opportunities.

| Traffic Type | Description | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Credit-based ads | Shown to active members in rotation | Free (earn credits) |
| Pro ad boost | Priority placement in rotation | Pro membership |
| Real Tracker referrals | Passive credits from widget installs | Free |
| Downline activity credits | Credits earned from referred members viewing ads | Free |

The traffic won't replace a well-optimized Google or YouTube campaign, but it's a legitimate source of free daily visitors — especially when you're starting out.

## Link Tracker and Analytics {#link-tracker}

The **Real Tracker** inside LeadsLeap tracks clicks on any URL and gives you:

- Total clicks and unique clicks
- Click duration (time spent on page — a quality signal)
- Geographic breakdown
- Device and browser data
- Bot filtering

The click duration feature is particularly useful. It filters out bots and low-quality traffic by measuring how long visitors actually stay on the destination page. This helps you compare traffic sources objectively.

Free members get 10 tracker slots. Pro members get unlimited trackers. If you're running affiliate campaigns across multiple platforms (traffic exchanges, solo ads, social media), the tracker helps you understand what's actually sending buyers versus what's sending bots.
