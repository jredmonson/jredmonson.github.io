## What Is a GHL Agency?

GoHighLevel — most people call it GHL — is an all-in-one marketing platform built specifically for agencies and the local businesses they serve. When you build a GHL agency for local business, you're creating a digital marketing business on top of GHL's infrastructure, then delivering that value to local owners who desperately need it.

Here's what makes the model work: you pay one platform fee and serve unlimited clients. Each client gets their own GHL sub-account — their own CRM, website, funnel, email and SMS system — and you charge them a monthly retainer for access and management.

That's the foundation of recurring income. Instead of chasing one-time projects, you build a client base that pays you every month because your systems run critical parts of their business.

## Core Services You Can Offer

When you build a GHL agency for local business, you're not locked into one offer. Here's a breakdown of the most in-demand services and what they look like in practice:

### 1. Local Business Websites

GHL includes a full website and landing page builder. You build professional websites for local clients in their sub-account, host them there, and charge a monthly maintenance and hosting fee.

**What this includes:**
- Mobile-optimized site with their branding and logo
- Contact forms that feed directly into their CRM
- Google Business Profile integration
- Embedded booking calendar (optional)

**Typical monthly charge:** $97–$197/month

### 2. Lead Generation Funnels

A simple 2–3 page funnel dramatically increases how many leads a business captures from their existing traffic. A free quote page, a free consultation offer, or a local service landing page with a form and an instant SMS response turns browsers into booked appointments.

**What this includes:**
- Custom funnel built in GHL
- Lead magnet or offer setup
- Automated SMS and email follow-up sequence

**Typical monthly charge:** $197–$397/month

### 3. Email and SMS Automation

This is where GHL truly earns its reputation. You set up automated campaigns that nurture leads, follow up with past customers, and send appointment reminders — without the business owner doing anything after initial setup.

**What this includes:**
- Welcome sequence for new leads
- Missed call text-back (saves businesses a huge amount of lost revenue on its own)
- Appointment reminders and confirmations
- Re-engagement campaigns for cold contacts in their database

**Typical monthly charge:** $147–$297/month

### 4. Reputation Management

Online reviews are everything for local businesses in 2026. GHL automates review requests — sending a text or email after a job or appointment asking for a Google review — and intercepts negative feedback before it hits public platforms.

**What this includes:**
- Automated review request sequences post-service
- Review monitoring dashboard
- Private negative feedback channel (unhappy customers contact you directly rather than posting publicly)

**Typical monthly charge:** $97–$197/month

### 5. Bundled Service Packages

Successful GHL agencies usually build tiered packages that bundle services together. A Starter package might be website plus reputation management. A Growth package adds lead funnels and SMS automation. A Pro package is the full stack with ongoing management.

| Package | Included Services | Monthly Price |

## Finding and Landing Local Business Clients

This is where most aspiring agency owners get stuck. The good news: local business clients are not hard to find. You just need a systematic approach.

### Pick Your Niche First

GHL agencies that scale fast almost always pick a niche. Instead of "I help all local businesses," pick one or two verticals and build case studies there:
- Home services (plumbers, roofers, HVAC, electricians)
- Health and wellness (chiropractors, dentists, med spas)
- Real estate (agents, property managers)
- Professional services (law firms, accountants, financial advisors)

Niching down makes your pitch sharper and your referrals more targeted.

### Where to Find Clients

| Source | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| Google Maps | Search your niche + city; businesses with few reviews are prime prospects |
| Facebook Groups | Local business owner groups — add value, don't pitch cold |
| LinkedIn | Connect with owners in your chosen niche |
| Fiverr | List your services professionally to capture inbound leads |
| Referrals | Happy clients send you their peers — ask for introductions |

### The Pitch That Works

Don't lead with "I'm a digital marketer." Lead with a specific problem you spotted.

Try: *"I noticed your business doesn't have an automated review request system — most of your competitors do. Businesses in [city] are losing 5–10 potential clients every month because their online reputation isn't working for them. Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if I can help?"*

Specific. Relevant. Outcome-focused. That's what gets responses.

## Pricing Your GHL Services

Pricing is one of the most common stumbling blocks for new GHL agency owners. Here's a simple framework to get it right:

**Know your floor.** What does it cost you to deliver the service? Include your time, GHL sub-account fees, and any additional tools.

**Price for outcomes, not hours.** A reputation system that generates 10 new 5-star reviews per month is worth far more than what it costs you to set up. Price accordingly.

**Start with an accessible entry offer.** A $197–$297/month package that includes a website and reputation management is easy for most local businesses to say yes to. Once they see results, upsell automation and lead funnels.

**Increase prices as you build case studies.** Your first clients may pay $197/month. Clients 10–20 should pay $397–$697/month. Your case studies justify the increase.
