## The Four Pillars in Practice

**Google Business Profile:** Your GBP is the foundation. Before citations, backlinks, or content can have their full effect, your profile needs to be completely and correctly optimized — right categories, completed attributes, keyword-informed description, seeded Q&A, structured photos.

**Local Citations:** Any online mention of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP). Manual submission across major and niche-specific directories with consistent NAP data signals legitimacy to Google. Automated bulk submissions often get NAP wrong and create inconsistency that suppresses rankings.

**Local Backlinks:** Links from locally-relevant or industry-relevant sites carry real ranking weight. Local news, chamber of commerce listings, industry associations, partner sites — these are permanent equity that keeps compounding.

**Published Content:** Content expands your keyword footprint beyond what your GBP can target directly. Service pages, location pages, and blog posts let you rank for the long-tail searches buyers use before they're ready to call.
