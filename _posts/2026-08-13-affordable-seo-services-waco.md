## What "Affordable" Actually Buys in Waco

At the $300-$500/month tier, an affordable SEO package in Waco typically covers on-page optimization (title tags, meta descriptions, page structure), Google Business Profile management, and a modest amount of monthly content - usually one to two pieces. This tier is realistic for lower-competition categories: landscaping, moving services, smaller home repair trades.

At the $500-$700/month tier, the package usually adds citation building (getting the business listed consistently across directories) and more frequent content, which matters more for higher-competition categories like HVAC, roofing, or emergency plumbing, where Waco's larger population means more businesses fighting for the same map-pack spots.

## Why Waco's Baylor Population Changes the Math

Baylor University adds a layer to Waco's search behavior that smaller towns in the region don't have to account for - a large, rotating population of students, faculty, and visiting families searching for local services on a different rhythm than a typical small-town market. This shows up most in categories like moving services, apartment-adjacent home repair, and food/hospitality, where search demand spikes around the academic calendar rather than staying flat year-round.

An affordable SEO plan that ignores this pattern - treating Waco like any other mid-sized Texas town - tends to underperform during the exact windows when demand is highest.
