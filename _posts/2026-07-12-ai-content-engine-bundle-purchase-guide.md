## The Single-Keyphrase Principle (And Why It's Not Optional)

This is the part that separates the bundle from a generic template pack, so it's worth spending real time on. The rule is simple: one post, one keyphrase, one measurable target. No post gets written until you can answer the question "what specific phrase is this supposed to rank for?"

This sounds restrictive, and it is — deliberately. Here's why that restriction is the point:

- **It kills scope creep.** Without a locked keyphrase, posts drift into covering five adjacent topics badly instead of one topic well.
- **It makes tracking possible.** You can't measure progress against "content about affiliate marketing." You can measure progress against a specific keyphrase's position in search results.
- **It forces research before writing.** You have to actually check search intent for that phrase before drafting, instead of guessing.
- **It gives AI tools a real anchor.** Prompts that reference a specific keyphrase produce tighter, more usable drafts than prompts that just say "write about X niche."

If you've ever published 20 posts and had no idea which ones were working, this is the failure mode the single-keyphrase rule is designed to prevent.
