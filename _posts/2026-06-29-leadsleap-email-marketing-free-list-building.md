## Common Mistakes New List Builders Make

**Mistake 1: Skipping the welcome sequence.** The highest open rates happen in the first 48–72 hours after someone subscribes. If your first email arrives five days later, you've lost the warm window. Set up at minimum a 3-email welcome sequence before you drive any traffic.

**Mistake 2: Sending only promotional emails.** A list that only receives sales pitches trains subscribers to ignore your emails. A simple ratio: two value emails for every promotional email. "Value" doesn't mean long — it means useful. A one-tip email that takes 60 seconds to read counts.

**Mistake 3: Waiting until the list is "big enough" to start sending.** There's no magic number. Start emailing at 10 subscribers. You'll learn faster with a real audience than by theorizing about what would work at 1,000.

**Mistake 4: Not tracking clicks.** LeadsLeap includes a link tracker. Use it. Knowing which offers your list clicks on — and which they ignore — is the data that improves future campaigns.

**Mistake 5: Abandoning the list during slow growth periods.** List building is slow at first, then it isn't. The compounding happens quietly. An affiliate marketer who sends consistent emails to a 200-person list for 12 months will outperform someone who built to 2,000 subscribers and then ghosted them for six months.
