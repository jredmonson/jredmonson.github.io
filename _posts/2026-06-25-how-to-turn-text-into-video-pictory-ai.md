## Step 2: Choose Script to Video {#step-2-choose-mode}

Click **Script to Video** on the dashboard. You'll land on a blank script editor with a few settings to configure before you paste anything.

**Set your aspect ratio first.** Choose from:
- 16:9 (landscape) — best for YouTube
- 9:16 (vertical) — best for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels
- 1:1 (square) — best for Instagram feed or Facebook

For a standard YouTube affiliate review video, choose **16:9**.

**Choose a template.** Pictory offers pre-built visual templates that control font style, transition effects, and color scheme. Pick one that fits your niche or brand. You can always change it later — don't overthink this step.

## Step 3: Paste Your Script and Generate Scenes {#step-3-paste-script}

Copy your script and paste it into the editor. Keep it to one idea per sentence — Pictory breaks your content into scenes sentence by sentence, and shorter sentences give it more flexibility to match visuals.

**Example script structure that works well:**

> Most people don't realize they're losing leads every time they miss a call.
> GoHighLevel fixes that with automatic text-back — it texts any missed caller within seconds.
> Here's how to set it up and why it matters for your business.

Each sentence becomes a scene. Pictory matches each one to stock footage from its library of millions of licensed clips.

When you're ready, click **Proceed**. Pictory takes 30–60 seconds to generate your initial storyboard.

## Step 4: Customize Visuals and Voiceover {#step-4-customize}

This is where most of your time goes — and it's the most important step for quality.

### Swapping Visuals

Pictory's auto-matched footage is good for generic topics but sometimes misses on specific ones. Review each scene in the timeline. If a visual doesn't fit, click it, search the stock library for a better match, and swap it out. You can also upload your own images or screen recordings as B-roll if your topic requires specific visuals (like a software walkthrough).

### Adding a Voiceover

Click **Voiceover** in the left panel. You have two options:

**AI Voiceover** — Pictory includes a library of AI voices in multiple accents and styles. Click through several to find one that fits your content. The quality is strong enough for YouTube and Pinterest without a professional recording setup.

**Upload Your Own** — If you want your own voice, record a simple audio file (even on your phone) and upload it. Pictory syncs it to your scenes automatically.

For beginners, the AI voiceover is the fastest path. Choose it, listen to the preview, and adjust the reading speed if needed.

### Setting Background Music

Pictory includes a royalty-free music library. Keep the volume low — music should be background texture, not competing with narration. Most affiliate review videos work best with soft, neutral background music or no music at all.

## Step 5: Add Your Branding and Captions {#step-5-branding}

### Captions

Click **Captions** and enable auto-captions. Pictory generates sync-accurate captions from your script. This matters for two reasons: accessibility (hearing-impaired viewers), and reach (most Pinterest and social media users watch videos with the sound off).

Adjust the font, size, and position so captions are readable without covering key visuals. Center-bottom is the standard safe zone.

### Intro and Outro

Add a simple text-based intro card (your video title) and an outro card with a call to action. Something like:

*Outro text: "Try Pictory free at [your link] — link in description."*

You're not putting your affiliate link inside the video itself — that goes in the video description when you upload it to YouTube or Pinterest.

## Best Practices for Affiliate Marketing Videos {#best-practices}

These are the habits that separate videos that generate clicks from ones that don't.

**Lead with a problem, not a product.** Start your script with the pain point your audience has, not the product you're promoting. "Tired of missing leads?" converts better than "Let me tell you about GoHighLevel."

**Keep videos under 5 minutes for affiliate content.** Attention drops sharply after 3 minutes for review-style content. Make your point, show the value, give the CTA, and stop. You can always make a follow-up video for deeper dives.

**One CTA per video.** Tell viewers exactly one thing to do next: "click the link in the description." Multiple CTAs split attention and reduce clicks.

**Publish consistently.** One video per week beats ten videos at once followed by silence. Algorithms reward consistency. So does your audience.

**Repurpose your best performers.** If a blog post drives traffic, turn it into a Pictory video. If a video gets traction on YouTube, make Pinterest pins from the stills. One piece of content, multiple distribution channels, compounding returns.

| Video Type | Best Platform | Ideal Length | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product review | YouTube | 3–5 min | Link in description |
| How-to tutorial | YouTube + Pinterest | 2–4 min | Link in description |
| Quick tip / teaser | YouTube Shorts, Pinterest | 30–60 sec | Watch full video / link |
| Blog repurpose | Pinterest video pin | 30–90 sec | Visit blog / affiliate link |
