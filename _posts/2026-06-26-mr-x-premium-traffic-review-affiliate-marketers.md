## Traffic Quality: What to Look For {#traffic-quality}

Not all paid traffic is equal. With any email traffic service, the key metrics to track are:

**Opt-in rate.** For a lead magnet landing page, a well-crafted page should convert at 30–50% of visitors. If you're seeing under 20%, the issue is usually your page, not the traffic.

**Email engagement.** Do the new subscribers open your follow-up emails? Low open rates (under 15–20%) after day one suggest the traffic doesn't match your offer niche.

**List quality over list size.** 100 targeted subscribers who open emails beats 1,000 disengaged ones. Track open rates, click rates, and ultimately conversions — not just subscriber count.

**Tier 1 traffic.** Look for services that specify Tier 1 countries (USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand). These audiences have higher purchasing power and generally convert better on digital offers.

The best way to evaluate any traffic source is to track it against a simple funnel: clicks → opt-ins → email opens → sales.

## How to Set Up a Campaign That Converts {#campaign-setup}

Getting value from any paid traffic source requires these fundamentals in place before you spend a dollar:

**Step 1: A focused lead magnet.** One specific, high-value freebie that solves a real problem for your target audience. Vague "free guide" offers underperform. Specific, titled freebies ("The 7-Step Affiliate Launch Blueprint") outperform.

**Step 2: A clean, fast-loading landing page.** One headline. One opt-in form. No navigation. No distractions. Your page needs to load in under 3 seconds. Use a simple tool like HighLevel or LeadsLeap to build it.

**Step 3: A follow-up sequence.** At minimum, a 5–7 day email sequence that delivers value, builds trust, and presents your offer. Traffic without follow-up is wasted.

**Step 4: Track everything.** Use UTM parameters or a link tracker to measure exactly how many clicks came from MR X, how many opted in, and which emails generated action.
