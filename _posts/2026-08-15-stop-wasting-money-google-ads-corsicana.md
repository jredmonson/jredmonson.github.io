## The Tracking Gap

Without proper conversion tracking - knowing which specific ad, keyword, or audience actually produced a call or form submission - a Corsicana business has no way to tell which part of an ad campaign is working and which part is pure waste. This is often the very first fix, since it turns guesswork into an actual feedback loop for improving the campaign.

## Keyword Targeting Mismatched to Market Size

A broad or highly competitive keyword strategy built for a bigger market can burn through a Corsicana-sized budget quickly without producing proportional results - Navarro County's search volume simply doesn't support the same keyword breadth that might work in Waco. Narrower, more specific local keyword targeting typically stretches a Corsicana ad budget significantly further.

## The Landing Page Problem

Even well-targeted ad traffic gets wasted if it lands on a generic homepage instead of a page built specifically to answer that search and drive one clear action - a dedicated landing page matched to the ad's specific offer or service typically converts meaningfully better than sending all ad traffic to the same general page.

## A Simple Audit Any Corsicana Business Can Run

Before assuming Google Ads simply "doesn't work" for a Corsicana business, it's worth checking three things directly: whether conversion tracking is actually installed and firing correctly, whether the campaign is set to target a reasonable radius around Corsicana rather than a broad, unfocused area, and whether the ad's destination page matches what the ad itself promised. Any mismatch in these three areas commonly explains most of what looks like "wasted" spend.

## Why Broad Targeting Wastes Budget Faster in Smaller Markets

A campaign targeting too wide a geographic area or too generic a keyword set burns through budget on clicks unlikely to convert - a mismatch that costs proportionally more in a market Corsicana's size than in a larger city where even inefficient targeting still captures enough volume to produce some results. Tightening geographic and keyword targeting to match Navarro County's actual market size is often the single highest-impact fix available.

## What Proper Tracking Changes About Ongoing Decisions

Once conversion tracking is correctly installed, a Corsicana business can start making informed decisions - pausing keywords that generate clicks but never conversions, and shifting budget toward whichever specific ads or search terms are actually producing calls. Without that tracking, every budget decision is effectively a guess.

**Should a Corsicana business pause Google Ads entirely if it seems to not be working?**
Not necessarily - pausing without first fixing tracking and targeting means never finding out whether the platform could actually work well with the right setup.

**How quickly can tracking issues typically be diagnosed and fixed?**
Often within a few days for the technical setup itself, though it typically takes another few weeks of data afterward to make confident, informed budget decisions.
