## What Is LeadsLeap? {#what-is-leadsleap}

LeadsLeap is a multi-tool platform built for online marketers. At its core, it combines four things in one place:

1. **A traffic cooperative** — members view each other's ads and earn credits to have their own ads seen
2. **An email list management system** — with autoresponders, broadcast emails, and forms
3. **A link tracker** — to measure clicks, conversions, and traffic quality
4. **A monetization layer** — through its affiliate program and ad network

What makes LeadsLeap unusual is that it has survived for nearly 18 years in an industry where most "free traffic" platforms disappear within two years. That longevity signals something is working.

The platform has a free tier and a Pro upgrade. The free plan is genuinely usable — not a crippled teaser.

## LeadsLeap Features Breakdown {#features}

Here's a closer look at what you get inside the platform:

### Ad Network / Traffic Co-op

Members place ads (banner or text) that are shown across the LeadsLeap network. You earn credits by viewing other members' ads (through the widget or member area), and spend those credits to get your own ads shown.

This is a traffic exchange at its core — meaning the traffic is from other marketers, not cold general audiences. That has implications for conversion rates, which we'll address below.

### SendSteed — The Autoresponder

LeadsLeap includes a built-in email marketing tool called SendSteed. It supports:

| Feature | Free Plan | Pro Plan |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Subscriber limit | 1,000 per list | Unlimited |
| Number of lists | 10 | Unlimited |
| Autoresponder sequences | Yes | Yes |
| Broadcast emails | Yes | Yes |
| Email open/click tracking | Yes | Yes |
| Automation rules | Limited | Full |

SendSteed is a legitimate email marketing tool. For marketers on a budget, it replaces the need for a paid autoresponder at the entry level.

### Real Tracker

LeadsLeap's link tracker shows you exactly who clicked your links, where they came from, how long they stayed on the page, and whether the visit was likely genuine. It's one of the more detailed free link trackers available.

| Metric Tracked | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| Clicks | Unique and total |
| Traffic quality | Real/bot detection |
| Average time on page | Yes |
| Geographic data | Yes |
| Conversion tracking | Yes (with pixel) |

### Page Builder

A basic funnel and landing page builder is included. It's not as polished as dedicated tools, but it works for opt-in pages, thank-you pages, and simple offer pages.

### Popups and Forms

Opt-in forms and exit-intent popups can be created and embedded on external sites. These connect directly to your SendSteed lists.

## The Traffic Component: What to Expect {#traffic}

This is where people get confused. LeadsLeap traffic is not the same as Google or Pinterest traffic.

**Who is seeing your ads?** Other LeadsLeap members — overwhelmingly affiliate marketers, make-money-online seekers, and online business builders. If your offer targets that audience, you can get decent results. If you're selling a niche product to a general consumer, results will be poor.

**Traffic quality is mixed.** The platform's "Real Tracker" exists precisely because some traffic in co-ops is low quality. LeadsLeap has worked to filter bot traffic, but you should track everything and not rely on raw click numbers.

**Best use of LeadsLeap traffic:** Opt-in pages for business opportunity offers, affiliate marketing training, list-building lead magnets, and tools for online entrepreneurs. If your offer fits the audience (internet marketers), the traffic converts. If not, treat it as supplemental rather than primary.

For building a broader traffic foundation, pair LeadsLeap with content marketing on Pinterest and YouTube — those platforms reach buyers who aren't already in the "make money online" space.
