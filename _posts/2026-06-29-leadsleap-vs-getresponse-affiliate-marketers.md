## Email Deliverability: Does It Matter Which You Choose?

Both platforms maintain solid sender reputations. Deliverability ultimately depends on your list hygiene, your content, and how you acquired your subscribers — more than which ESP you use.

That said, a few practical points:

- **GetResponse** has been at this longer and has established relationships with major inbox providers. Their deliverability track record is excellent.
- **LeadsLeap** uses its own infrastructure. Deliverability is generally good, but you may occasionally need to warm up a new list more carefully.

For most affiliate marketers sending to an engaged list, the difference is negligible. Where it matters is if you're sending high volumes to cold or semi-cold lists — in which case either platform will flag you if your content is aggressive.

Neither tool protects you from sending bad emails to bad lists. That's a you problem, not a platform problem.

## The Traffic Advantage: LeadsLeap's Unique Edge

This is the section that genuinely separates the two tools, so it warrants real attention.

LeadsLeap operates a credit-based traffic cooperative. As a member, you earn credits by spending time viewing other members' ads (via the AdWidget or browser add-on). You can then spend those credits to show your own ads to other members.

As a Pro member, your ads run passively — you earn credits even when you're not actively browsing. The result is a low-cost, semi-automated traffic stream pointed at a network of online marketers.

This isn't viral traffic or search traffic. It's a warm, marketer-focused audience that already understands email lists, affiliate products, and digital tools. If you're promoting something relevant to that audience — and as an affiliate marketer, you probably are — the traffic converts at rates you won't get from cold solo ads.

GetResponse has no equivalent. It's purely a send-side tool. You bring the audience; it helps you communicate with them.

For a new affiliate marketer with no audience yet, this matters enormously. LeadsLeap gives you a mechanism to start building a list while you're simultaneously learning the email marketing side. GetResponse assumes you already have traffic figured out.

## When GetResponse Makes More Sense

I said LeadsLeap wins for most affiliate marketers under 5,000 subscribers. That's not a universal statement.

GetResponse makes more sense when:

- You're running webinars as a core part of your business — LeadsLeap has no webinar feature
- You need deep integrations with e-commerce platforms like Shopify or WooCommerce
- Your automation flows are complex — GetResponse's visual workflow builder is genuinely superior
- You're managing a team with multiple users and need role-based access
- You want polished, designed landing pages with more template variety

At a certain scale, the sophistication of GetResponse's automation pays for itself. If you're segmenting audiences across multiple products, running re-engagement sequences, and A/B testing subject lines at volume, GetResponse gives you finer control.

But that's a later-stage problem. Most affiliates reading this comparison are trying to get from zero to a functioning list and their first consistent commissions — not managing enterprise automation.
