## What are my immediate steps after a plumbing emergency at home?
Immediately stop the problem from getting worse, document the situation, and call a 24-hour plumber. Swift action reduces water damage and can preserve insurance coverage.

Recommended immediate actions (numbered):
1. Turn off the main water shutoff valve to stop flow into the house.
2. If safe and relevant, shut off electricity to affected appliances or areas (especially if water is near outlets or the electrical panel).
3. Call a 24-hour plumbing service and describe the emergency clearly (burst pipe, sewer backup, no hot water).
4. Move valuables and furniture out of the affected area and place buckets to collect water.
5. Take clear photos and videos of the damage and keep receipts for any emergency purchases.

Insurance and documentation facts:
- Homeowner policies commonly cover sudden and accidental water damage but generally exclude damage from long-term leaks or neglect; check your insurer’s policy language.
- Keep the plumber’s written report and invoices — insurers and legal advisors often require these for claims or disputes.

## What are my options besides calling a 24 hour plumber and how do they compare?
You can call an emergency 24-hour plumber, schedule a regular-hours plumber, or attempt a DIY fix for minor issues. Each option has trade-offs in speed, safety, and risk.

| Option | When to use | Speed | Risk |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 24-hour emergency plumber | Burst pipes, major leaks, sewer backups, gas/heater failures | Fast (may be immediate) | Low professional risk, higher cost for emergency call |
| Scheduled plumber during business hours | Small leaks, planned upgrades | Next-day or later | Lower cost, delayed repair risk |
| DIY temporary fix | Small, contained leaks if you have skills | Immediate | Potentially high if misdiagnosed; may void warranties |

Guidance:
- Call a 24-hour plumber for any flooding, sewage backup, or when health/safety is at risk.
- Schedule regular-hours service for non-urgent repairs and for getting multiple written estimates.
- Only DIY minor fixes (tighten a fitting, replace a shutoff valve washer) if you have confidence and tools; document everything.

Emergency call fees and time-and-materials charges vary widely by provider and the nature of the repair, so ask for an explanation of the emergency call fee, hourly labor rate, and parts mark-up before work begins. ### How long will a 24 hour plumber take to fix my burst pipe or sewer backup?
Repair time depends on the problem’s severity; simple repairs can take under an hour, while major repairs or replacement of sections of piping can take several hours to multiple days. The plumber should give an estimated timeframe after the initial assessment and provide options for temporary stabilization and permanent repair.

### Is it worth calling a 24 hour plumber for a small leak at night or can I wait until morning?
If a leak is small, contained, and not causing property damage, you may be able to shut off the water and wait; however call an emergency plumber if the leak is increasing, near electrical systems, or if sewage is involved. When in doubt, a brief phone call to an emergency plumber to describe the situation can help you decide.

### What documents or proof should I get from the plumber in case of a dispute or insurance claim?
Get the plumber’s name, company, license number, a detailed written invoice, photos of the repair area after work, and any warranty information for parts and labor.
