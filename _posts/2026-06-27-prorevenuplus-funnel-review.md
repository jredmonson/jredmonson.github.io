## How the Rebranding Works {#how-rebranding-works}

Once you access the system, you receive instructions for substituting your affiliate IDs into the funnel assets. The process typically involves:

1. Getting your unique affiliate links for each product featured in the funnel.
2. Replacing the default links with your links in the provided materials.
3. Uploading the email sequence to your autoresponder (LeadsLeap, GetResponse, Aweber, etc.).
4. Directing traffic to the opt-in page.

The rebrandable pages may be hosted on the ProRevenuePlus platform or may be materials you load into your own funnel builder. Review the specific setup guide for current instructions when you access the system.

**Important:** Rebranding a done-for-you funnel does not make it a unique asset. Many marketers are running versions of the same system. Your differentiation has to come from your traffic source, your email list relationship, and any additional value you layer on top — like your own lead magnets, bonus offers, or follow-up content.

If you approach this as "set it and forget it," results will be limited. If you treat it as a foundation you build on, it becomes significantly more effective.
