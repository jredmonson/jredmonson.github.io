## Buda's Growth Means a Different Starting Point Than Georgetown

Where Georgetown's estate planning market includes a lot of long-time residents updating existing plans - particularly in Sun City Texas - Buda's market skews toward first-time planners who moved to the area recently for its newer housing developments and relative affordability compared to central Austin. For this group, the estate planning conversation often starts from zero: no existing will, no named guardian for children, no power of attorney in place at all.

That starting point actually makes the decision simpler in one respect - there's no old document to reconcile or update, just a plan to build from scratch. But it also means there's no safety net if something happens before the plan gets done, which is the argument for treating this as a near-term priority rather than a someday project.

## Hays County Probate Court and Growth-Driven Delays

Buda's estate and probate matters are handled through the Hays County court system, based in San Marcos - a separate court from both Travis County (Pflugerville, Austin) and Williamson County (Georgetown, Round Rock). Hays County's rapid population growth has also meant a growing court caseload, which can mean longer timelines for probate matters to move through the system compared to a decade ago. An attorney who works in Hays County regularly will have a realistic sense of current timelines, rather than assuming the process moves as quickly as it might have in the past.
