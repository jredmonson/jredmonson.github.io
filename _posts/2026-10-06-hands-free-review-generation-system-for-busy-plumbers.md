A hands free review generation system for busy plumbers is a set of tools and workflows that automatically requests reviews from customers after a job, so technicians don’t have to chase feedback. It uses SMS or email triggers tied to job completion, templates that make leaving a review simple, and links directly to your Google Business Profile to capture feedback quickly.

## What is a hands free review generation system for busy plumbers?
A hands free review generation system for busy plumbers is an automated process that requests reviews on behalf of technicians and office staff. It removes manual follow-up by sending timed SMS or email requests, tracking responses, and nudging customers who haven’t yet left a review.

Key components:
- Automated triggers tied to scheduling or invoicing (for example, after marked \"job complete\").
- Short, friendly SMS and email templates with one-click review links.
- A dashboard to track sent requests, responses, and completed reviews.
- Integration with your point-of-sale, CRM, or scheduling software.

Three quick setup steps:
1. Connect the system to your scheduling or invoicing tool (or use a manual CSV import).  
2. Add short SMS and email templates with a direct Google review link.  
3. Set triggers (for example: send SMS 1 hour after job marked complete, follow-up email after 48 hours if no review).

## How long does it take to set up and start seeing reviews?
You can set up a basic hands free review generation system in about 30–90 minutes and often see the first review within 24–72 hours after sending the first batch of requests. A minimal setup—claiming your Google Business Profile and creating message templates—can be completed in under an hour; connecting more systems or testing templates may extend that to a few hours.

Practical timing notes:
- Claiming or updating your Google Business Profile is free and typically takes less than 30 minutes.  
- Send the first automated SMS within 1 hour of completing a job to maximize response rates; follow-ups at 24–48 hours are effective.  
- Expect to iterate on wording for 1–2 weeks to find messages that maximize conversions without being pushy.

## What are the best practices to get customers to leave authentic reviews?
Best practices focus on timeliness, simplicity, and ethics: ask when the customer is happiest, make the process one tap, and never offer incentives for reviews that violate platform rules. Good timing and short messages increase completion rates.

Actionable best practices:
- Ask within 1 hour to 24 hours of a successful job completion.  
- Use an SMS with a one-click Google review link or a short email with clear CTA.  
- Keep messages under 160 characters for SMS, and use natural language like: \"Thanks for having us today — can you tap this link to leave a quick review?\"  
- Follow platform rules: do not offer payment or discounts in exchange for positive reviews.  
- Train dispatchers and techs to request permission: a quick in-person ask raises the chance a customer will respond to the automated message.

Templates to test (short):
1. SMS: \"Thanks for choosing [Your Company]. Quick favor: can you tap this link to leave a short review? [link]\"  
2. Email subject: \"How did we do today?\" with two-sentence body and direct link.

### How to keep reviews authentic and compliant
- Never edit a customer’s review content or incentivize a specific rating.  
- If a customer reports a problem, route that to customer service first rather than asking for public feedback. This protects your public reputation and resolves issues offline.

Realistic expectations:

## What types of automation should I prioritize for a plumber?
Prioritize automations that align with your workflow: job completion triggers, SMS-first outreach, and a simple escalation for non-responders. These three automation types cover the bulk of review capture success.

Priority automations:
1. Job completion trigger: automatic SMS 1 hour after job marked complete.  
2. First follow-up: automated email 24–48 hours later if no response.  
3. Escalation: a second SMS reminder after 3–5 days, then manual outreach if still no response.

Small checklist to get started:
- Claim and verify Google Business Profile.  
- Create one short SMS and one email template.  
- Set a trigger tied to your scheduling or invoicing system.  
- Test with 10–20 recent customers, measure response, and tweak messaging.

Short, checkable facts to keep in mind:
- Google Business Profile is free to claim and should be the primary destination for review links.  
- You can set up a basic automated workflow in three steps (connect, create templates, set triggers).  
- A basic setup often takes 30–90 minutes depending on integrations.  
- Send review requests within 24 hours of service for the best chance of a quick response.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How much does a hands free review generation system cost for a small plumbing company?
Costs vary by approach: many SaaS review platforms start under $20 per month for basic automations, while full-service or agency options commonly run several hundred dollars per month. Expect additional per-SMS fees if your plan does not include them.

### How long does it take to see a meaningful increase in reviews after setting one up?
You can see the first reviews within 24–72 hours after sending automated requests; a meaningful, steady increase in review volume typically becomes noticeable within 4–8 weeks as the system consistently asks after each job.

### Is it worth automating review requests instead of asking customers in person?
Yes—automation ensures every satisfied customer is asked even when techs are busy, and it scales with your business. In-person asks are great for immediate wins; combine both for the best results.

### What is the alternative if I don't want automation for reviews?
Alternatives include manual follow-ups by office staff via phone or email or focusing on in-person requests after each job. These can work for very small teams but often consume more staff time and yield fewer consistent results.
