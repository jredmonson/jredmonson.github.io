## What Makes Round Rock's Estate Planning Needs a Little Different

Round Rock's employment base includes a large share of tech and corporate professionals with equity compensation - stock options, RSUs, or employer stock purchase plans - that a generic will template often handles poorly or not at all. An estate planning attorney familiar with this kind of compensation can make sure those assets are actually accounted for rather than left in a gray area.

Round Rock is also home to a lot of young families who bought their first home in the area's newer subdivisions along the SH-45 and I-35 corridors. For that group, the estate planning conversation often starts with guardianship - who raises the kids if both parents are gone - which is a decision a will formalizes but a lot of families haven't actually made yet, even informally.

Round Rock estate matters go through the same Williamson County probate court as the rest of the county, based in Georgetown.
