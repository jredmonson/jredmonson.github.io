## What Is Hostinger Horizons?

Hostinger Horizons is an AI-powered website builder launched by Hostinger, one of the world's largest web hosting companies. Where traditional builders like Wix or Squarespace ask you to start from a template and drag elements around, Horizons takes a different approach: you describe what you want in plain text, and the AI builds a working site draft for you.

For context, Hostinger already hosts millions of websites and is well-known for aggressive pricing in the shared hosting market. Horizons is their move into the AI builder space — and it arrives with the infrastructure and support network of a company that has been doing this for over a decade.

The core promise is simple: describe your business or project, and get a functional website in minutes without touching a line of code.

## How the AI Generation Works

The workflow is straightforward:

1. You enter a prompt describing your site (e.g., "I run a landscaping business in Tampa, Florida. I need a homepage, services page, and contact form.")
2. Horizons generates a full site structure with written copy, imagery, and layout.
3. You review and refine using a point-and-click editor.
4. You publish to Hostinger's infrastructure with one click.

The AI handles the heavy lifting that typically bottlenecks non-technical users — writing placeholder copy, selecting a layout, and organizing the navigation. Even if you've never built a website before, you can get to a presentable draft within a single session.

You can also iterate. If the first generation doesn't match your vision, adjust your prompt or use the editor to swap sections, update text, and change colors. The AI can regenerate individual sections without rebuilding the whole site.
