## What a Transparent Quote Actually Looks Like

A transparent quote for a Hillsboro business names the exact deliverables at each price point: how many pieces of SEO content per month, whether Google Business Profile is actively managed, whether citation building is included, and whether reputation management means actively requesting reviews or just monitoring them passively. Two providers can both quote "$600/month for SEO" and mean completely different amounts of actual work - transparency is what closes that gap before a contract gets signed, not after.

## No Hidden Fees: What to Watch For

The most common place hidden costs sneak into an otherwise reasonable-looking quote is the setup fee - a one-time cost that should cover real build work (a new website, initial SEO foundation, tracking setup) but sometimes gets padded or repeated disguised as a "re-setup" fee later. A transparent provider explains upfront exactly what the setup fee buys and confirms it's genuinely one-time.

## What to Expect in the First 90 Days

A transparent Hillsboro retainer should include a written 90-day outline at signing - not just a monthly price, but what specifically happens in month one (setup and foundation), month two (initial content and optimization), and month three (first measurable results review). A provider unwilling to commit to that kind of outline in writing is a sign the pricing may be less transparent than it sounds.
